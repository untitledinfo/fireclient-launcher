Removal requests

Use this page to report copyright problems, ask for a page to be removed or corrected, report a fake download, or ask about your data.

How to contact us

Message a server owner in the FireClient Discord, or open a GitHub issue that only asks for a private way to send your notice. Do not post personal details, access tokens or private documents in public.

FireClient Discord GitHub issue

Copyright (DMCA) notice

If you believe something on fireclient.net or in a FireClient release infringes your copyright, send us:

  • What the copyrighted work is
  • The exact page address or file you object to
  • Your name and a way to reach you
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent or the law
  • A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner
  • Your signature (a typed name is fine)

We review notices and may remove or disable the material while we do. If you think material was removed by mistake, you can send a counter-notice with your contact details and a statement explaining why.

Remove or correct a page

If a page names you, states something that is not true, or you want it taken down, send the page address, what is wrong and how you would like it changed.

Report a fake or harmful download

The genuine installer is only linked from the download page and the official releases. If you find an installer, video or site that pretends to be FireClient, send us the link so we can report it to the host.

Your data

  • This website: it has no accounts. Items saved in your browser (language choice, ad notice choice, release cache) can be cleared in your browser settings.
  • The launcher: data stays in the .fireclient folder in your Windows user folder. Uninstalling leaves that folder, and you can delete it yourself in File Explorer.
  • Community features: if you used the optional community account features, that data is held by that service. Contact it under its own policy.

Details are in the Terms and Privacy notice.

Search engines

Removing a page from this site does not remove it from Google or Bing at once. They update their results when they next visit. You can also use their own removal tools to hide an outdated result sooner.
