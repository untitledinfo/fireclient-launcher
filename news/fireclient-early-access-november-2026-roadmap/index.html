FireClient early access is live, full version planned for November 2026

Early access is open for Windows. Here is what works today, what is still planned, and what we know about the full version.

On this page
  1. What you can use today
  2. What is planned
  3. Full version in November 2026
  4. How to follow along

FireClient early access is live for Windows 10 and 11. This post is the short version of where things stand and what comes next.

What you can use today#

Early access includes the launcher for Minecraft Java Edition and the Fabric in-game client for Minecraft 1.21.1:

  • A draggable HUD with eleven elements and three accent themes
  • A Modrinth mod manager, with room for your own jar files
  • A worlds library that shows each world's icon, last-saved date and size
  • A memory setting that is passed to Java as the max heap
  • Local skins, and a Paper server you can run from the launcher

What is planned#

A crosshair editor and cosmetics are planned but not built yet. They are not part of early access.

Full version in November 2026#

The full version is planned for November 2026. We do not have an exact release day to share yet, and plans can change, so we will confirm the date on the early access page, in the Discord and in the RSS feed before it happens.

How to follow along#

Join the FireClient Discord for questions and bug reports, subscribe to the RSS feed, or add a November reminder from the early access page. If you have not tried it yet, download FireClient.
