2. Safety & Anti-Malware Certification

We believe in total transparency. Every release of FireClient is subjected to rigorous automated and manual security verification before reaching players:

  • 100% VirusTotal Clean: FireClient releases are submitted to and verified against 70+ leading antivirus scanning engines on VirusTotal with zero malicious detections.

3. Credential Security: Windows DPAPI Vault

Your Minecraft and Microsoft accounts are your property. FireClient strictly adheres to industry gold-standard credential handling practices:

  • Direct Microsoft OAuth 2.0: Sign-in is handled directly through Microsoft/Xbox Live official endpoints. Your Microsoft account password is never seen, handled, or stored by FireClient.
  • Hardware-Tied Windows DPAPI Encryption: Refresh tokens saved in accounts.json are encrypted using the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) keyed exclusively to your Windows user account. Even other users on the same computer cannot decrypt your session tokens.
  • Zero Remote Token Logging: FireClient does not transmit tokens, auth headers, or passwords to any remote FireClient server or third party. All authentication tokens strictly communicate with login.microsoftonline.com and api.minecraftservices.com.
  • Offline Guest Mode: Players without internet connectivity or using offline profiles can launch seamlessly without requiring any network ping or Microsoft credential exchange.

4. World, Mod & File Protection

FireClient treats your game assets and creative work with extreme care:

  • Non-Destructive Operations: The launcher never deletes your Minecraft worlds, resource packs, screenshots, or shader packs during updates or uninstallation.
  • Safe Sandboxing: Each version instance operates in an isolated environment, preventing mod conflicts from corrupting standard Minecraft installations.
  • Automatic Conflict Detection: The launcher actively detects mismatched mod builds and prevents startup crashes before they affect your worlds.
