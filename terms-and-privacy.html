Privacy

Local storage. FireClient keeps launcher preferences, installed profiles, worlds, resource packs, mods, screenshots, logs, offline skins, and account data in the user's .fireclient folder (or an existing .justice-launcher folder retained from an earlier install). Microsoft account credentials in accounts.json are protected using Windows Data Protection API for the current Windows user. The installer and uninstaller intentionally leave this game-data folder in place.

Microsoft sign-in. If online sign-in is used, authentication requests go to Microsoft, Xbox, and Minecraft services. Those providers receive the network and account information needed to authenticate and operate their services. FireClient stores the account token data locally in protected form; do not share your Windows account or launcher data folder.

Downloads and version checks. Installing or checking Minecraft, Java, loaders, mods, resource packs, shaders, servers, or launcher updates contacts the relevant provider, which may include Mojang/Microsoft, Adoptium, Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, Modrinth, PaperMC, GitHub, and download hosts/CDNs. These requests expose normal connection data such as IP address and request metadata to those providers. Mod-library lookup may send local file hashes to Modrinth to identify public mod projects.

Community features. When the optional FireClient/Justice community account features are used, the app connects to justiceclient.org. Information entered or used for those features (such as account name, profile, friends, messages, server listings, screenshots, and marketplace actions) is sent to that service to provide the requested feature and is subject to that service's policies.

Discord presence. When Discord Rich Presence is enabled and Discord is running, FireClient sends game presence to the local Discord application. Presence can include the Minecraft version and, when available, the server name/address shown by the feature. Discord handles that information under its own policies.

Logs and diagnostics. Game output and crash logs are kept locally for the user. FireClient does not upload a crash log through the launcher. A diagnostics summary is copied only after the user chooses the copy-diagnostics action; the user chooses where to paste or share it.

The installer does not delete saved worlds or resource packs. Uninstall removes the installed launcher program but leaves the .fireclient game-data folder, which can be removed by the user separately through Windows File Explorer.

Website ads and cookies. This website may show Google AdSense ads. Ads load only after you accept the notice on the site. Google and its partners may then use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads; see how Google uses data. If you decline, no ad code loads. The launcher itself shows no ads.

This website

What the site collects. The website has no accounts, forms or comments, and it does not ask for your name or email address.

Analytics. The site may use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages are visited. See Vercel's documentation for what it collects. The site also uses Google Analytics (Google tag) to measure visits, such as which pages are viewed and from which country. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics cookies stay off until you accept the notice. Google Analytics may set cookies and sends this usage data to Google; see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Change your analytics choice.

Browser storage. The site saves a few small items in your browser's local storage: a short cache of release information, your language choice (and whether you closed the language suggestion), and your choice on the ad notice. These stay in your browser and are not sent to us. You can clear them in your browser settings.

Language suggestion. To offer a page in your language, the site reads your browser's language list on your device. It is not sent anywhere.

Third-party requests. To show release details and community numbers, your browser contacts GitHub, the site's own release function hosted on Vercel, and Discord. Those services receive your IP address and normal request details under their own policies. Download buttons lead to GitHub. Share buttons open the chosen service only when you click them.

Hosting logs. The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs under its own policy.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data, such as access or deletion. The website does not keep accounts or profiles, so there is normally nothing to delete on our side. See Removal requests for how to ask.

Removal and takedown requests

To report copyright or other content, ask for a page to be removed or corrected, or report a fake download, see Removal requests.
